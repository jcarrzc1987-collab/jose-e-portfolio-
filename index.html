What I built

Feature highlights
Booking request form

Pick a car, pickup and return times, and lounge pickup or delivery. It checks that return comes after pickup and shows the rental length.

Filterable fleet

Twelve cars sorted into Corvettes, Exotics, Luxury SUVs and Everyday, each with a photo gallery and a "Check dates" shortcut.

Protection plans

Three optional plans matched to each car class, laid out so the differences are clear at a glance.

Cinematic intro

A neon logo intro made in pure CSS, with Skip and Replay. It plays once per visit and stays off for people who prefer reduced motion.

Trust built in

Google rating and real reviews up front, plus an FAQ that answers age, deposit and insurance questions before anyone has to call.

Local SEO

Business details marked up for Google, plus link previews so the site looks right when shared by text or social media.

The challenge
Sell an experience, not a rental counter

America's Motors has a guest lounge, delivery and a 24-hour schedule. The site had to show that personality and still make booking quick.

The approach
Night-drive visual identity

A near-black background, a neon red accent, and tall condensed headlines that feel like a dashboard. Real photos of the cars, the lounge and the building carry the story.

The result
Built for the phone in your hand

Every section works on a phone, with tap-to-call and text buttons always nearby and a booking form that fits on one screen.

The challenge
Make taxes feel less scary

People often arrive stressed, holding an IRS letter. The site needed to feel warm and steady while showing serious credentials.

The approach
Desert calm, handwritten warmth

Warm charcoal and cream, a sand accent, an elegant serif with handwritten notes, and a mesa-and-sun line drawing that sketches itself in when the page opens.

The result
A guided path to help

Visitors pick their situation and get pointed to the right service, then book a consultation in a couple of taps.

Fraunces + Outfit + Caveat
Tabbed services

21 services sorted into four tabs: Individuals, Businesses, IRS Problems and Advisory.

"Where do I start?" finder

Five plain-language choices, like "I got a letter from the IRS," each with a matched recommendation.

Tax deadline calendar

The six key federal dates for the year, shown as calendar tiles.

Credentials up front

A credentials strip and an "Why an Enrolled Agent" section that build trust before the first call.

Virtual assistant

A chat helper with a waving sloth mascot answers common tax questions, hides any Social Security or account numbers typed by mistake, and hands visitors off to Jaimee with their question already filled in.

Consultation form

Fills in an email with the visitor's details, and reminds them not to send sensitive information.

About me

I'm Jose Carrasco, a Phoenix-based, certified software engineer who builds software for any field. Whether it's a booking flow for a car rental business, a product page for a SaaS company or a service finder for a tax practice, I learn how the industry works and engineer around it.

I specialize in Anthropic development: building AI-powered features, assistants and integrations with Anthropic's API, alongside the websites they live in.
